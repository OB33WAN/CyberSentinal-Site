A scam simulator that plays like a game.

Every scenario is built to mislead, pressure and test the player. Learning arrives after the choice, so the player feels the consequence first and remembers the lesson.

Texts, email and social traps

SOC red and blue team play

Practise attack and defence decisions with small mission briefs, operator controls and evidence-led outcomes.

Built around decisions, not lectures.

The game avoids long answer screens during simulations. Players progress by doing: answering, refusing, checking, reporting, escalating or making the mistake.

Designed with safer training boundaries.

Cyber Scam Simulator is a sandbox. It does not ask players to enter real passwords, bank details, recovery phrases or one-time codes.

Privacy-aware by default

The current Android build does not declare internet, microphone, camera, location or contacts permissions. Progress is saved locally on the device.
